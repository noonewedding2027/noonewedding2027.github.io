Our Story

Daniel & Patrycja met in March of 2023 online (surprisingly as they were never "online-dating people") from almost 2 hours away! After both searching for quite some time, they had low expectations that anything "real" could come out of these apps, but they figured what did they have to lose and started talking. Little did they know this would lead to deep conversations right off the bat, and even multiple facetime calls lasting up to 7 hours long (they did NOT feel that long!) before they had ever met in person. They both felt as though that was the longest they had smiled for in a very VERY long time.

Ultimately, they had such a good time talking to eachother...literally nonstop... that they agreed to meet eachother in person for the first time in Oswego, NY where Patrycja went to school at the time. Driving from Geneseo, Daniel pulled in and couldn't believe his eyes as the most beautiful girl he had ever laid eyes on was standing there waiting for him to pick her up. From there, they went to dinner at Kiyomi (a Japanese restaurant in Oswego), and then went on a walk along the river — it was so cold outside, they just HAD to hold hands to stay warm. They then spent the rest of the night talking and playing UNO, at which Patrycja kicked Dan's butt (which she still does to this day *shhhh*).

Following this amazing first date, Patrycja and Dan would drive almost 2 hours one way to see eachother pretty frequently. Since things were going so well, and they were noticing a very strong connection developing, Daniel thought it was about time to make it official and ask Patrycja to be his girlfriend. The date was April 23rd, 2023 — Daniel and Patrycja were together in Oswego, so he thought this was a perfect time to ask her. However, knowing exactly what Daniel was going to ask, Patrycja stopped him and said: "Wait until tomorrow, because 23 is an ugly number." — Daniel obliged, and waited to pop the question until the 24th, to which Patka responded, "Make me a sandwich!". To this day, Patrycja claims she has never received her sandwich...they have since been inseparable.

Since then, Patrycja and Daniel have essentially done everything together.
